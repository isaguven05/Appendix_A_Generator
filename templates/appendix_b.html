5 Image Filename Guide

Appendix B images must encode the provider, technology, frequency, metric and building directly in the filename — exactly the format produced by the RF extraction tool. The app reads this to select the right template slide and sort the output automatically.

📋 Required filename format
PROVIDER_TECH_MHZ_METRIC_BUILDING.png EE_LTE_1800_RSRP_TICKET_HALL.png
THREE_5GNR_3500_SSRSRP_MIDWAY.png
VMO2_GSM_1800_RXLEV_PLATFORM_1_2.png
VODAFONE_UMTS_2100_RSCP_BASEMENT.png
🏷️ PART 1 — Provider prefix

Must be the very first part of the filename, followed by an underscore:

ProviderPrefixExample
EE EE_ EE_LTE_1800_RSRP_TICKET_HALL.png
THREE THREE_ THREE_GSM_1800_SNIR_MIDWAY.png
VODAFONE VODAFONE_ VODAFONE_LTE_2100_SNIR_PLATFORM.png
VMO2 VMO2_ VMO2_5GNR_3500_SSRSRP_TICKET_HALL.png
📡 PART 2 — Technology, Frequency & Metric

Use exact values below (case-sensitive). Only these combinations are valid:

TechFreq (MHz)MetricFull example
GSM1800RXLEV or SNIREE_GSM_1800_RXLEV_MIDWAY.png
UMTS2100RSCP or ECIOEE_UMTS_2100_RSCP_TICKET_HALL.png
LTE1800RSRP or SNIREE_LTE_1800_RSRP_BASEMENT.png
LTE2100RSRP or SNIREE_LTE_2100_SNIR_CONCOURSE.png
LTE2300RSRP or SNIREE_LTE_2300_RSRP_STAIRS.png
LTE2600RSRP or SNIREE_LTE_2600_SNIR_PLATFORM.png
5GNR3500SSRSRP or SNIREE_5GNR_3500_SSRSRP_MIDWAY.png
🏢 PART 3 — Building name

Everything after the metric, with spaces replaced by underscores. Slides are sorted in this default order:

TICKET_HALL → MIDWAY → BASEMENT → STAIRS → CONCOURSE → PLATFORM

Any other building name is placed at the end alphabetically. Multi-word names: PLATFORM_1_2, LIFT_MOTOR_ROOM, LOWER_CONCOURSE

⚠️ Files that WILL be skipped
  • No recognised provider prefix — e.g. LTE_1800_RSRP_TICKET_HALL.png
  • Unrecognised tech/freq/metric combo — e.g. EE_LTE_900_RSRP_MIDWAY.png (900 MHz not in template)
  • Fewer than 5 parts when split on underscores
